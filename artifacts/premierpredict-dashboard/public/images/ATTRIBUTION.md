# Matchup visual assets

Captain figures are AI-generated, stylized full-body illustrations inspired by
public footballers. They are not photographs, confirmed matchday appearances,
live camera feeds, model inputs or endorsements.

Club-captain names and reference seasons were checked on 4 October 2026:

- Current-season references: https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4706912/premier-league-club-captains-for-202627-season
- Marked 2025/26 references for Burnley, West Ham and Wolves:
  https://www.sportingnews.com/uk/football/news/who-each-premier-league-teams-club-captain-202526/40c8917dc93dc6b3a80295fe

Official club emblems are cached from the Premier League badge service:
`https://resources.premierleague.com/premierleague/badges/100/t{club-code}.png`.

project is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by those organisations.
